# 08 — Implementation Roadmap

## Faz 0 — Governance
Kaynak izinleri, policy registry ve veri sözlüğü.

## Faz 1 — Acquisition
Adaptör arayüzü, raw snapshots, evidence store, drift detection.

## Faz 2 — Identity
Canonical player IDs, entity resolution, collision tests.

## Faz 3 — Normalization
Metric registry, units, scopes, canonical candidates.

## Faz 4 — Validation
Reconciliation, confidence, conflicts, verified store.

## Faz 5 — Deterministic Analytics
Per90, ratios, trends, availability, data quality.

## Faz 6 — Benchmarks
League/position/role/age cohort percentiles.

## Faz 7 — Feature Engines
Position-specific current ability and tactical vectors.

## Faz 8 — Youth Engine
Development velocity, senior transition, potential range.

## Faz 9 — LLM
Input contract, output schema, evidence binding, eval set.

## Faz 10 — Reporting
Final report JSON, UI/PDF renderer, publication gates.

## Faz 11 — Backtest
Historical players, potential calibration, scout feedback.
