# Position Renderers

GK: goal frame, save map, PSxG, crosses, distribution.  
CB: duel, aerial, box defense, progression.  
FB: overlap/underlap, crossing, recovery.  
DM: recovery, pressure resistance, rest defense.  
CM: circulation, progression, coverage.  
AM: pockets, creation, through balls.  
WINGER: role split, wide/half-space, dribble, shot.  
ST: box movement, shot quality, runs, link play.

Tematik ikonlar scoring'e etki etmez.
